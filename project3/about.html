About This Website

This website looks at animal cruelty across different spaces in human society: the ocean, laboratories, farms, wild habitats, entertainment industries, and even our homes. Instead of hiding violence, the project brings it to the surface so we have to confront how easily it is normalized and ignored.

Content And Intention

Throughout the site, animals are treated not as background decoration but as beings whose bodies are constantly reshaped by human desire—for entertainment, profit, food, fashion, convenience, or tradition. The project asks a simple question: What if we could no longer look away? What if everyday choices and systems were seen from the animals’ perspective, not just our own comfort?

This is not a complete map of all animal suffering, and it does not offer easy solutions. Instead, it aims to create a space of discomfort and reflection, where viewers notice their own position inside these networks of harm and consider how their habits, politics, and empathy might change.

Trigger Warning

Many pages contain content and images of violence, confinement, and neglect. The video section in particular includes real footage of abuse. Please take care of yourself while exploring this site: pause, step away, or skip sections if you feel overwhelmed.
